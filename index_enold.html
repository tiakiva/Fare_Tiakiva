PRACTICAL TIPS

  • Sea bath: Shoes are recommended. For swimming, I suggest a small spot to your left, in front of the gendarme houses, as there is a channel with fine sand where you can use your mask for a snorkeling session, very pleasant.
  • Near the accommodation: The pension Cécile, just a stone's throw away, offers a buffet with a Polynesian show on Thursday evenings. A pizza truck is located 50m from the accommodation, just exit to your left after the small bridge.
  • Here is some useful information regarding the accommodation and its particularities due to its location on a coral atoll:
    • Water: The particularity of atolls is that there are no mountains or rivers. To supply the accommodation with water, we have two solutions. The first is drawn directly from a brackish, non-potable water lens, used only for the toilets and the tap at the entrance at the bottom of the steps. You can safely use it to rinse off. The second comes from the rain, which we collect and store in two cisterns you’ll see when you arrive at the house. Therefore, use it sparingly. It is mainly used for cooking and showering. The washing machine is not included in the rental and is only used for washing household linen (sheets, towels) for water-saving reasons.
    • Waste: Two bins are available for your use. The green one is for recyclable waste (paper, cans, water bottles), and the gray one is for non-recyclable waste (soiled items, milk cartons, plastic packaging). Your food scraps will make the fish in the lagoon happy. Collection is done by the roadside according to the following schedule: The gray bin is collected on Mondays and Fridays, while the green bin is collected only on Wednesdays.
    • Hot water: Hot water is provided by a water heater, which is activated by a switch in the kitchen. It is the first switch from the left, labeled for easy identification. It should be in the 'up' position to be activated. Normally, 20 minutes are enough to heat the tank, which has a capacity of 50 liters of hot water. Be sure to turn it off after use.
    • Wi-Fi and TV: We have a good-quality internet connection thanks to fiber optics. The Wi-Fi network is called "Faretiakiva". You can also enjoy television through Amazon and Disney+ channels.

HOUSE RULES


  • In Polynesia, we take off our shoes when inside. Please make sure to remove them before entering the house.

  • Smoking is prohibited inside the accommodation. However, you can set up by the lagoon to smoke. A garden table is provided, where you can sit and enjoy the view.

  • Do not use kitchen utensils to try to open coconuts.

  • Animals are prohibited inside the house.

  • The rental is limited to 5 people. The extra charge is 50 Euros per additional person.

  • Bikes are available and are not for rent. Be sure not to leave them out in the rain or under the sun after use.
