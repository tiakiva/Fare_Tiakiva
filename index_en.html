PRACTICAL TIPS

  • Sea swimming: wear water shoes. A good spot for snorkeling is to the left, across from the gendarme houses.
  • Nearby: Pension Cécile offers a Thursday buffet with a Polynesian show. A pizza food truck is 50m away to the left after the small bridge.
  • Water: Brackish water (non-drinkable) is used for toilets and outdoor tap. Rainwater is collected for showers and kitchen. Washing machine is reserved for linen only.
  • Waste: Two bins: green (recyclables), grey (non-recyclables). Food leftovers can be given to the lagoon fish. Grey bin: Monday/Friday, Green bin: Wednesday.
  • Hot water: Controlled by switch in kitchen (first one on the left). Takes 20 mins for 50L. Turn off after use.
  • Wifi and TV: Good fiber connection. Network name: "Faretiakiva". Access to Amazon and Disney+ included.

HOUSE RULES

  • In Polynesia, we take off our shoes indoors. Please do so before entering.
  • Smoking is not allowed indoors. You may smoke by the lagoon where a garden table is available.
  • Do not use kitchen utensils to open coconuts.
  • No pets allowed.
  • Rental limited to 5 people. €50/person for extra guests.
  • Bicycles are available (not included in rental). Do not leave them in the rain or sun.

Thank you for staying! Feel free to contact me for any questions.
