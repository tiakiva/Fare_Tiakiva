Practical Tips

  • Sea bath: Shoes are recommended. I suggest swimming to the left, in front of the gendarme's houses, as there is a channel with fine sand in the sea, very pleasant.
  • Nearby accommodation: The Cécile guesthouse, just a few steps away, offers a buffet with a Polynesian show on Thursday evening. A pizza food truck is located 50 meters from the accommodation, to the left after the small bridge.
  • Here are some useful information regarding the accommodation and its unique characteristics due to its geographical location on a coral atoll:
    • Water: The particularity of atolls is that there are no mountains or rivers. We have two solutions for providing water to the accommodation. The first is drawn directly from a brackish water lens, non-potable, and is used only for the toilets and the tap at the entrance at the bottom of the steps. You can safely use it for rinsing. The second comes from rainwater, which we collect and store in two cisterns you will see as you approach the house. Use it sparingly. It is mainly used for cooking and showering. The washing machine is not included in the rental and is only used for cleaning household linen (sheets, towels) for water conservation reasons.
    • Waste: Two bins are provided. The green one is for recyclable waste (paper, cans, bottles, etc.), while the grey one is for non-recyclable waste (soiled waste, milk boxes, plastic wrappers). Your meal leftovers will make the lagoon's fish happy. Collection is done by the roadside according to the following schedule: The grey bin is collected on Monday and Friday, and the green bin is collected only on Wednesday.
    • Hot water: Hot water is provided by a water heater that is activated from the electrical box in the kitchen. It is the first switch from the left, marked with a label. It must be in the upward position to be activated. Normally, 20 minutes are enough to heat the tank, which has a 50-litre hot water capacity. Be sure to turn it off after each use.
    • WiFi: We have a good quality internet connection thanks to fiber optic. The WiFi network is called "Faretiakiva".
